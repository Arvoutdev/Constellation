HOW ARVITURE WORKS

Three steps between "stuck" and confident.

Step One

We diagnose the gap

A short placement check identifies exactly where a student is struggling — not just "math," but fractions, or balancing equations, or reading comprehension pacing.

Step Two

We match a tutor

Students are paired with a tutor selected for that specific gap, their grade level, and how they learn best — visual, verbal, hands-on, or a mix.

Step Three

We track the progress

Every session logs what was covered. Parents get plain-language updates, and the worksheet library adjusts to reinforce whatever comes up in tutoring.

TAILORED MATCHING

Not "a tutor." The right tutor.

Most tutoring services hand you a generic profile and hope for the best. Arviture matches on the details that actually affect learning: subject sub-topic, grade band, learning pace, and even personality fit.

OUR STORY

Built by tutors who got tired of one-size-fits-all.

Arviture started in a school library, not a boardroom. We believed every student's gap is specific — so the support should be too.

OUR MISSION

Give every student a tutor who actually fits.

Arviture Accelerated Learning exists to close the gap between "my kid needs help" and "my kid is getting the right help." We do that by pairing students with tutors chosen for their exact subject needs, and by backing every family with a worksheet library deep enough to reinforce what's covered in each session.

We're not trying to replace teachers or schools. We're trying to be the most useful thing that happens between report cards.

HOW WE GOT HERE

From one tutoring room to a national platform.

2018

A single tutoring room in Ann Arbor

Arviture opened as a small after-school tutoring room serving 30 local students, matching them with tutors by hand on a whiteboard.

2020

The worksheet library goes online

What started as a filing cabinet of practice sheets became a searchable digital library, opened up to any family — tutoring client or not.

2022

Matching goes algorithmic

We built our diagnostic-to-tutor matching system, replacing the whiteboard with a process that accounts for subject sub-topic, pace, and learning style.

2026

210+ school district partnerships

Today Arviture supports families directly and partners with school districts to extend tutoring access to students who need it most.

WHAT WE VALUE

Specificity over averages

PRICING FAQ

Common questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Families can move between Practice, Guided, and Immersive at any time, and changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

What happens if the tutor match isn't working?

You can request a new tutor at no additional cost. Most families see a new match confirmed within 48 hours.

Is the worksheet library really unlimited?

Yes, on every plan. There's no cap on how many worksheets a student can download or print in a month.

Do you offer discounts for multiple children?

Immersive plans include up to three student profiles at no added cost. Larger households can contact us for a custom quote.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

No. All plans are billed monthly and can be canceled anytime before your next billing date.
